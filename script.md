### What are breaches/how do they happen
A data breach is an unintentional release of information and can occur in many ways, with varying intent and severity. Data breached through deliberate attacks can result from phishing, ransomware, theft, exploit hacks, leaks, bribery, and espionage. While the greatest range of kinds of breaches are deliberate, many breaches, historically, are accidental. Accidental breaches can occur when access, data, or credentials are misplaced or lost and consequently fall into the wrong hands. Additionally, because the internet and data are incredibly interconnected, data can be breached from a company, organization or other without direct fault of the data holder. Third-party service providers like AWS, CrowdStrike or others can experience breaches themselves, and through no fault of their own, clients experience data breaches because of an exploit used in a service provider.

While the term data breach is used to describe all different types of actions and events, it’s important to clarify and understand the differences of each kind.

### History of data breaches
Data breaches, while devastating to those affected, are still incredibly prevalent despite modern cybersecurity technologies and practices. Already, in September 2026, there were seven publicly confirmed data breaches affecting over forty-two million accounts, with data ranging from full names to vehicle registration plates included in the breaches.

One of the largest confirmed data breaches in history happened at Yahoo in 2013 when over three billion records were affected. While the breach occurred in 2013, it wasn’t confirmed publicly until 2016, after over three years had passed. Yahoo acknowledges that names, email addresses, telephone numbers, dates of birth, hashed passwords and, in some cases, encrypted or unencrypted security questions and answers were stolen in the breach. The timing of the breach affected Yahoo while it was in the process of selling part of its company and ended up reducing the sale price of Yahoo’s main internet business by three hundred million dollars.

Other significant data breaches in history include First American Financial in 2019, where over eight hundred eighty-five records were exposed; Marriott International in 2018, where over three hundred eighty million records were exposed; and, more recently, Nation Public Data in 2024, where nearly three billion records were exposed.

With the severity and frequency of data breaches, it’s likely you or someone you know has been affected and has had personal information accessed without their knowledge or consent.
Future of breaches/cybersecurity

### The Future of Data Breaches
In the future, data breaches may become more dangerous because of new technology. First, hackers can use AI to create more realistic phishing emails and cyberattacks. Second, as more personal information is stored online, the risk of data breaches may increase. However, technology can also help prevent these attacks. For example, companies can use AI security systems to detect suspicious activities and respond faster. Overall, technology can create both new risks and better protection.

### How to Protect Yourself from Data Breaches
There are several simple ways to protect ourselves from data breaches. First, we should use strong and different passwords for each account. Second, enabling multi-factor authentication adds extra security, even if someone steals our password. Third, we should avoid suspicious emails and links because they may be phishing attacks. Finally, keeping our software updated can fix security problems. Although we cannot prevent every data breach, these simple steps can greatly reduce our personal risks.
